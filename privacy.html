Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Solitaire does not require an account. Game progress, settings and statistics are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Solitaire uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising data, IP address and interactions with advertisements to provide, measure and improve advertising, subject to your consent choices and applicable law.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent and provide privacy choices relating to advertising. Available choices may depend on your region and applicable law.

Third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Their handling of information is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policies.

Local game data

Game progress, settings and statistics are stored on your device. The app does not require you to create an account to use these features.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or services it uses change. The latest version will be published here.
