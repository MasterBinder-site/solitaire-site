Solitaire Support

Need help with Solitaire? You can find answers to common questions below.

How do I start a new game?

Choose Play from the home screen and select Draw 1 or Draw 3.

What is Daily Game?

Daily Game gives you a daily Solitaire challenge. Hints are not available in Daily Game.

Can I continue an unfinished game?

Yes. Your current game is stored on your device and can be resumed using Continue.

Does Solitaire require an account?

No. Solitaire does not require an account.
